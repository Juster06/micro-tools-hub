Privacy & limits

One static JPEG/PNG/WebP ≤5 MiB, 4096 per side and 8 million input pixels. User dimensions10–100 mm, margins0–30 mm, gaps0–20 mm, 1–40 tiles; A4/Letter at 150/300 DPI, ≤10 million sheet pixels and 40 MiB PNG. At most 2 decimal places for millimetres. Raster edges round independently to the nearest pixel (≤0.5 pixel per edge); nominal photo widths can differ by1 pixel after quantization. Transparent input is composited onto white. Calibration ruler reserves 12 mm. Print the whole sheet at 100%/actual size with fit-to-page disabled; measure the 100 mm ruler. The PNG embeds nominal DPI as pixels-per-metre metadata. PNG viewers may ignore resolution metadata, so use the fixed-mm print layout or set physical sheet dimensions explicitly. No jurisdiction-specific dimensions, official passport-photo acceptance, biometric/face/pose validation or guaranteed physical printer accuracy.
