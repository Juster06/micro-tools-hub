Privacy & limits

Static PNG ≤5 MiB, 4096 per side and 8 million decoded pixels. Select 1–6 sizes: 16, 32, 48, 64, 128, 256; ICO ≤1 MiB. Native browser PNG frames are validated as square 32-bit RGBA with correct chunk CRCs before packing the reviewed ICO directory. Transparency and aspect ratio preserved with padding; resampling may soften small artwork. Intended for modern PNG-capable ICO readers, not legacy BMP-only compatibility. No upload or external encoder; native encoder/decode deadlines approximately 10 seconds.

Inputs stay in this browser tab; no uploads or saved drafts. Hosting and the existing styling CDN receive ordinary page requests. Optional site analytics requires consent. Privacy and cookies.
