Rules, limits & privacy
Meeting title required (up to 160 UTF-16 units), date YYYY-MM-DD and 1–50 agenda items. Item names are required (up to 200 units); optional presenters are up to 80 units. Duration is 1–1,440 whole minutes per item and total is at most 10,080 minutes. Reorder and remove buttons change the displayed order. Print uses the currently generated agenda. Input and output remain local and are never saved automatically.
Hosting and the existing Tailwind styling CDN are separate network requests; meeting content is not uploaded or stored.
