Rules, limits & privacy

Static PNG only, up to 5 MiB; input dimensions 1–4096 pixels and at most 8 million pixels. Watermark is one line of up to 100 UTF-16 units, with five positions, 0–100% opacity, 8–256 px font and black or white color. Browser font rendering may vary; text may be clipped on small images. PNG export retains transparency outside the overlay. This visible watermark does not prevent copying. Metadata is not deliberately copied but no metadata-removal guarantee is made.

Hosting and the existing styling CDN are separate network requests. User input is not uploaded by this tool.
