Conversion rules, counts & privacy

Seven styles, predictable rules

  • UPPERCASE / lowercase: native Unicode casing. Accents and non-Latin text are retained.
  • Title Case: lowercase first, then capitalize significant words. Keep a, an, the, and, as, at, but, by, for, from, in, nor, of, on, or, per, to, via and vs lowercase, except the first/last word of each line or after a colon.
  • Sentence case: lowercase first; capitalize the first letter and after . ? ! or a line break. Common abbreviations, initials and decimal points receive limited protection. Names/acronyms and ambiguous sentence endings need your review.
  • Capitalize Words: lowercase first, then uppercase the first letter of each word. Internal straight/curly apostrophes stay within a word; hyphens separate words. No special surname rules.
  • Alternating Case: start lowercase and alternate across cased letters. Numbers, spaces, punctuation, emoji and letters without case do not advance the pattern.
  • Inverse Case: flip each cased letter; leave other characters unchanged.

What the counters mean

Characters count Unicode code points, including spaces and line endings. Emoji sequences and combining accents can count as several characters. Words count non-whitespace groups, not linguistic words. Empty input has zero lines; otherwise each CRLF, CR or LF starts another line, including a trailing empty line.

Unicode & limits

Whitespace and punctuation are preserved. No accent stripping or Unicode normalization is applied. Language-neutral casing can expand a letter (ß → SS); Turkish, Greek context and editorial conventions may need manual review. Limit: 100,000 UTF-16 code units. Oversized input is kept and a clear error is shown.

Your text stays here

Conversion runs in this browser tab. No input is uploaded, logged or stored. Styling loads from Tailwind’s Play CDN. Copy depends on browser permissions; downloads are UTF-8 text files. Text areas use the browser’s normal LF line endings.
