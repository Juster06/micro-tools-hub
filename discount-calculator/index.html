Exact rules, limits & privacy

Default: two decimal places. Original price must be nonnegative; discount is inclusive 0–100%. Savings = price × discount / 100; final price = price − savings. Zero price, zero discount and a 100% discount are valid. The report includes original price, percentage, savings and final price. Amounts are rounded independently and displayed sums may differ after rounding. No currency symbol, exchange rate, tax, additional fee or cash-rounding model is assumed. Each numeric field accepts a complete ASCII decimal, optional sign/exponent and trimmed surrounding whitespace; no grouping, currency/percent symbols, hex, NaN or Infinity. Each field is capped at 64 UTF-16 units; magnitudes at most 1e12 and, unless zero, at least 1e-12. Nonfinite or result magnitudes above 1e15 fail clearly. JavaScript IEEE-754 arithmetic applies; rounded output uses native toFixed with 0/2/4/6 decimals (default 2), normalizing displayed negative zero. Values such as 1.005 can expose native binary rounding. Inputs are preserved; no truncation. Output is bounded below 1,000 units and is not arbitrary-precision/accounting software.

No user-input uploads, tracking, saved drafts or external processing. Page/style delivery uses hosting and the existing Tailwind Play CDN; inline fallback keeps this page usable without the CDN. Existing homepage analytics is separate from this utility’s input processing. Clipboard availability depends on browser support/permission; denied/unavailable copy selects the current output with manual guidance. UTF-8 text export uses a fixed filename and releases temporary URLs even on failure.
