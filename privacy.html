Privacy and cookies

Everyday Digital Box provides browser-based utilities. Tool inputs are processed in your browser; the tools do not intentionally upload your input to our servers. Some features you choose, such as copying or downloading, use your device or browser.

Optional analytics

With your permission, we use Google Analytics to understand page visits and improve the site. Before you accept, we do not load the Google Analytics tag. Analytics receives page information and ordinary network information such as your IP address and browser information. We configure page locations without query strings or fragments and do not intentionally send tool inputs.

Your choice

Choose Accept analytics or Reject analytics in the banner. Your choice is remembered for 180 days when storage is available; an expired preference is removed on your next visit. Use Privacy settings on any tool or the homepage to change your choice or withdraw consent. If storage is unavailable, your choice applies only to the current page and you may be asked again. Withdrawal stops future analytics initialization and attempts to remove first-party Analytics cookies; requests already sent cannot be recalled.

Other services and storage

Our hosting provider receives ordinary page requests, which can include IP addresses and browser headers. Some pages load styling or other resources from third-party content-delivery networks, including jsDelivr; those providers receive requests for those resources independently of analytics. Individual tools explain any optional storage they use. Browser settings can block scripts, cookies or storage, which may affect appearance or remembered preferences.

Links to other services open sites governed by their own privacy practices.
